\begin{abstract}\noindent
We relax the equality of measurement-error correlations for true dyad states 01 and 10. The resulting channel has six measurement parameters, compared with five for the pooled model and twelve for an unrestricted channel. We evaluate 800 fresh simulated networks under asymmetric and symmetric dependence, using paired audit budgets. In the primary asymmetric scenario, the Six/Full RMSE ratio for structural reciprocity is \SameRatio{} \SameCI{} at equal budgets and \HalfRatio{} \HalfCI{} at half the audit budget. The respective prespecified retention rules are \SameResult{} and \HalfResult{}. We separately report channel recovery, prior sensitivity, and numerical diagnostics. The results concern the specified synthetic mechanisms and do not establish external validity on real networks.
\end{abstract}
\section{Model and prospective design}
The earlier five-parameter channel pools dependence across true states 01 and 10. Directional asymmetry violates this restriction. The present study isolates the effect of removing it, while preserving the structural model, shared marginal error rates, and inference procedure. Correlated misclassification and internal validation have established precedents \cite{roy,tang}, as does inference from noisy network measurements \cite{newman}. We study a specific parameterization and its empirical tradeoffs.

True labels in unordered dyad $d$ follow reciprocal probit:
\[
Y_{dk}=\mathbf1\{x_{dk}^{\mathsf T}\beta+\epsilon_{dk}>0\},\qquad
\epsilon_d\sim N_2(0,R_\rho),\qquad R_c=\begin{pmatrix}1&c\\c&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]
For $t\in\{00,01,10,11\}$, measurement flips satisfy $E_k=\mathbf1\{Z_k\leq\Phi^{-1}(r_{t_k})\}$, with $Z\mid Y=t\sim N_2(0,R_{\kappa_t})$, $r_0=\alpha$, and $r_1=\gamma$. Observations are $O=Y\oplus E$. With $a_k=2(t_k\oplus o_k)-1$,
\begin{equation}
C_{t,o}=\Phi_2\!\left(a_1\Phi^{-1}(r_{t_1}),a_2\Phi^{-1}(r_{t_2});a_1a_2\kappa_t\right).
\end{equation}
\emph{Six} has parameters $(\alpha,\gamma,\kappa_{00},\kappa_{01},\kappa_{10},\kappa_{11})$. Setting $\kappa_{01}=\kappa_{10}$ nests \emph{Five}. \emph{Full} has twelve free row probabilities. Including three regression coefficients and $\rho$, total dimensions are 10, 9, and 16, respectively. For fixed binary margins, each row has one coupling degree of freedom. The Gaussian copula spans its interior Fr\'echet range, giving the six-dimensional family with shared FP/FN margins. It does not permit direction-dependent marginal error rates. Unequal discordant correlations require a fixed direction convention.

For random gold-label audits $A$, we use the collapsed likelihood
\[
L=\prod_{d\notin A}\sum_t p_d(t;\beta,\rho)C_{t,O_d}\prod_{d\in A}p_d(Y_d;\beta,\rho)C_{Y_d,O_d}.
\]
We optimize the joint posterior in transformed coordinates, including Jacobians, and map the joint-Hessian marginal intervals back to native scales. These are mapped modes, not posterior means. Priors are $\rho,\kappa_t\sim U(-1,1)$, $\alpha,\gamma\sim .5\,\mathrm{Beta}(2,8)$, and $\beta\sim N(0,(X^{\mathsf T}X/(2m))^{-1})$, where $X$ stacks both directed covariate rows and $m$ is the dyad count. Full rows use $\mathrm{Dirichlet}(.5,.5,.5,.5)$. Priors are not information-equivalent.

\textbf{Independent replication.} Each case contains 400 new networks with $n=90$, $m=4{,}005$, $\beta=(-.2,.6,1.1)$, $\rho=.5$, FP $=.06$, and FN $=.22$. Two non-intercept covariates are independent standard normals. Asymmetric truth uses $(.75,-.65,.45,.20)$, seeds 1010000--1010399; symmetric truth uses $(.75,-.25,-.25,.20)$, seeds 1020000--1020399. Reusing earlier parameter settings deliberately tests replication of the identified mechanism with fresh data. Independent RNG streams generate covariates, structural truth, measurement errors, and audit order. The generator uses normal draws independently of the fitted CDF.

Audits reveal 100 or 200 dyads (200 or 400 directed gold labels), with nested subsets and identical data across methods. There are 800 independent networks, not 1,600 independent network-budget replicates. The protocol and source hashes froze before generation. This is an internal prospective plan, not public preregistration. No fitting initialization uses unaudited truth.

\section{Structural performance and audit burden}
Two primary contrasts compare Six(200) and Six(100) with Full(200), under asymmetric truth. Retention requires an upper 97.5th percentile of the paired RMSE ratio $\leq1.10$, a lower two-sided 95\% Wilson coverage bound $\geq.90$, and empirical numerical failure rate $\leq1\%$. Nominal coverage is 95\%. We use 10,000 paired network bootstrap samples with seed 132017. The one-sided $.025$ allocation to each primary RMSE claim gives an approximate Bonferroni allocation across both claims. Failed fits count as noncoverage, and finite estimates remain in RMSE. The coverage gate is a practical tolerance rather than a test of exact 95\% calibration.

\textbf{Scope.} This model retains common directional FP/FN rates, covariate-homogeneous measurement channels, independent unordered dyads, and random error-free audits. It omits node effects, transitivity, and direction-dependent marginal errors. Six requires a scientifically meaningful orientation of the two directions; swapping directional labels while fixing parameters changes its error law. Gold-label audit strata identify conditional error probabilities in the population when true states have positive probability, without guaranteeing finite-sample precision. Four hundred replicates improve Monte Carlo resolution but cannot establish universal audit savings. Reusing parameter settings with independent seeds establishes replication within the simulated family, rather than external validation on a new domain. A single posterior diagnostic dataset cannot establish general calibration of approximate intervals.

The contribution is the six-parameter channel and the measured cost--accuracy tradeoff. General correlated misclassification and internal validation predate this work. The implementation independently reconstructs the reciprocal-probit framework from a supplied group report; the original authors' source code was unavailable and their report is not redistributed.

\textbf{Reproducibility.} The \texttt{yichao\_dev} branch contains the protocol, model and analysis code, source/design hashes, compressed per-network fit records, summaries, posterior draws and diagnostic attempts, editable \LaTeX{} sources, and build instructions. Numerical estimates and manuscript tables derive from those records. The study is separate from SGDiff training benchmarks. Source checks, numerical tests, and PDF render checks accompany the archived results.
