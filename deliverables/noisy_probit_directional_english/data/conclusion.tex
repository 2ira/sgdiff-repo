\textbf{Main interpretation.} The two primary rules are both met. The clearest incremental result is a 24.7\% reduction in channel-entry RMSE relative to Five at 200 audits. Structural RMSE improves by 1.0\%, and Five also meets the half-budget criterion on these data. This replication therefore does not establish that six parameters are necessary for audit savings.

Under symmetric truth, the descriptive Six/Five RMSE ratio is 0.995 [0.992, 0.997]. No structural RMSE penalty is observed here. However, Five and Six differ in prior geometry: the mean of two independent uniform correlations has a triangular prior, whereas the pooled correlation in Five is uniform. Small structural gains therefore do not isolate flexibility from prior effects. The asymmetric negative correlation remains difficult to estimate: at 200 audits, the mean mapped mode for $\kappa_{01}$ is approximately $-.440$, versus the true $-.65$. Improved channel RMSE does not establish unbiased latent-correlation recovery.
