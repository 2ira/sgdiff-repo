\textbf{Increment beyond Five.} At 200 audits, the descriptive Six/Five structural RMSE ratio is 0.990 [0.983, 0.997], a 1.0\% improvement. Channel-entry RMSE decreases by 24.7\%. Five also meets the half-budget rule on these fresh data (ratio 1.032 [0.976, 1.092]). Consequently, passing that rule cannot by itself establish a benefit specific to the additional parameter. These secondary intervals have no multiplicity adjustment.
