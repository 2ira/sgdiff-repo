\subsection{Node-importance-conditioned AUROC}

To align with the team's node-rank AUROC protocol, we used the same pinned evaluator on our saved predictions. For each dataset and seed, Degree and PageRank are computed on the clean training-positive graph; tied ranks are resolved by SHA256 of the dataset, seed and node ID. At each integer $k=0,\ldots,100$, we retain original test pairs with at least one endpoint among the top $\lceil Nk/100\rceil$ nodes. All models and perturbations use the same fixed cohort. AUROC is pooled over retained positive and negative pairs; $k=0$ is undefined and $k=100$ exactly reproduces the original test AUROC. Three seed estimates are summarized with their sample standard deviation. Points with fewer than 20 positives or 20 negatives are flagged as low support.
